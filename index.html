Hi, I'm Fia.

I'm Irish, I live in Manchester, and I've been working my way through its charity shops since I arrived here in 2020 — twenty-three, a mature student, with very little money and a lot of opinions about clothes.

I grew up somewhere without much fashion culture, which meant I spent years looking at clothes online before I could ever buy any. It turns out that's quite a good education.

These days most of the photographs are taken by Rachel, who I live with and who has got seriously into her camera. Between us we own a scruffy van we converted ourselves, and we spend most weekends driving it to towns we've never been to, because the charity shops there have things nobody in Manchester has seen.
